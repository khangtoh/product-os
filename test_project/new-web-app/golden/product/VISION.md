# Vision

Updated: 2026-09-27.

## Durable ambition

A researcher can recover something they saved while reading, without ever having organized it.

Source of direction: the project owner, onboarding round 1 on 2026-09-27. The audience it serves — researchers who save articles mid-reading and later lose track of them — is the owner's explicit, untested hypothesis, not validated user evidence. The ambition stands as product intent; the audience claim stands as a hypothesis to test.

## Principles and boundaries

- **Retrieval must not depend on organizing.** Findability cannot rest on folders, tags, or structure the user has to maintain. This is the distinguishing commitment against bookmarks and notes apps, and it constrains any future feature that would make retrieval conditional on filing work.
- **Saving happens mid-task.** The save path must stay cheap enough to use without breaking out of reading.

Durable non-goals, unacceptable tradeoffs, and strategic boundaries beyond these are not yet established; they are scheduled for a later onboarding round. Repository-level constraints (no dependencies, no analytics or third-party scripts, no build step) are recorded under Boundaries in the canonical contract.

Product identity stays canonical in [../PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md).
