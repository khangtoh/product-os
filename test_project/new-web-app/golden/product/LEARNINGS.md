## Rules

- State the population, context, or version for which a lesson holds.
- Link supporting and contrary evidence; distinguish correlation from a causal result.
- Explain what changed: a priority, product behavior, evaluation, strategy, or runbook.
- Record confidence and what would overturn the lesson.
- Preserve superseded or contradicted lessons with their replacement links; do not silently erase them.
- Promote repeated operational failures into runbook or process changes. A lesson without a changed decision or an explicit decision to retain the current approach has not closed the loop.
